# 00 — Vision and products

## The institute in one paragraph

As-Sunnah Dawah & Research Institute (ASDRI) trains thoughtful, well-read dawah workers and researchers who can answer contemporary intellectual confusion (scientism, secularism, atheism, feminism, orientalism, LGBTQ/gender discourse) with research-based, Quran-and-Sunnah-grounded responses. It runs a 3-year flagship programme for young ulama (PYS + specialisation), a 6-month certificate and a 2-year diploma for university graduates, and short programmes (Arabic teacher training, Ramadan dawah training, Azan training, research methodology). It is fully funded by As-Sunnah Foundation's Zakat Fund; students who qualify study, live and eat free.

## Why the website exists (the client's actual goals, in priority order)

1. **Credibility and recruitment.** A prospective student (a qawmi-madrasa graduate or a university graduate) must be able to understand each course, judge eligibility, and apply online. Admission notices today go out only on Facebook.
2. **Donor trust.** Donors, especially diaspora, should see exactly where Zakat goes, sponsor a specific student, get a receipt instantly and a progress report later.
3. **Intellectual dawah reach.** Research articles, fatwa answers, refutation content, journals and videos should be findable, readable and citable, and should outlive Facebook's feed.
4. **Operational relief.** Notices, FAQs, downloads and a fatwa queue should reduce repeated phone calls and messages to staff.

## Audiences

| Audience | Comes for | Must be able to |
|----------|-----------|-----------------|
| Prospective student (alem) | PYS, Ramadan training, Azan, teacher training | Read course, eligibility, dates; apply; track application |
| Prospective student (general graduate) | CCIS → Diploma | Same, plus understand the path CCIS → Diploma → next steps |
| Donor (local) | Zakat, sadaqah, scholarship | Pay with bKash/Nagad/card in 1 minute; get receipt; calculate zakat |
| Donor (diaspora) | Zakat in USD/SAR/EUR | Pay internationally; recurring; sponsor a student; see progress |

| Researcher / scholar | Journals, call for papers, faculty publications | Read in-browser, cite, download, submit |
| Institute staff | Everything | Edit all content; manage notices, applications, fatwa queue, donations; toggle modules |
| Student / alumni (later) | Portal | Login, see own records, alumni directory |

## Visible products

1. **Public website** — Bangla default, English second. Nine top-level sections (see IA).
2. **Admin dashboard** — Payload CMS admin: content, media, notices, courses, faculty, publications, fatwa workflow (submitted → assigned → answered → published), applications, donations and receipts, campaigns, site settings and feature flags, users and roles.
3. **Admissions portal** — public application form per course intake; applicant account to see status (submitted / shortlisted / written exam / viva / admitted / not selected) and download admit card.
4. **Donor portal** — login (email OTP), donation history, PDF receipts, recurring pledges, sponsored student's anonymised progress, thank-you messages.
5. **Student / Alumni portal** (phase 4) — login, profile, results and attendance (source for sponsor progress reports), alumni directory.

## Success criteria for launch (phase 1)

- Every page in the IA exists with real client content or a designed placeholder.
- A visitor on a mid-range Android phone can read a course and submit an application in under 5 minutes.
- A donor can donate to any of the four funds and receive a PDF receipt by email within a minute.
- Staff can publish a notice with a PDF attachment and status badge in under 2 minutes without a developer.
- Lighthouse ≥ 90 on Performance, Accessibility, SEO, Best Practices for home, a course page, a notice, a blog post.
- The site does not look like a template. Someone who knows the Foundation's work should feel it is "theirs".
