[Heading3] 3. Vision & Core Objectives
Vision Statement: Preparing thoughtful, knowledgeable, and effective dawah workers and researchers to address contemporary intellectual confusion and social fitnah, providing academic responses to misconceptions against Islam.
Core Pillars:
Authentic Quran-Sunnah-based intellectual dawah.
Bridging traditional Islamic sciences with modern disciplines (Psychology, Philosophy, Media, etc.).
Character tarbiyah, leadership, and public engagement.
[Heading3] 4. Featured Programs (Academics Preview)
Card grid featuring active key courses with quick details ([ Duration ], [ Eligibility ], [ Learn More ]):

Preparatory Year for Specialization (PYS): 3-Year residential flagship program for talented young Ulama focusing on higher dawah & modern challenges.
Certificate Course in Islamic Studies (CCIS): 6-Month foundational course in Tajweed, basic Arabic, Fiqh, and Sirah for university graduates.
Diploma in Dawah & Islamic Studies: 2-Year (4 Semesters) advanced diploma for CCIS graduates covering deep classical and modern dawah tools.
Arabic Language Teacher Training: 15-Day intensive pedagogy and modern CLT-method training for Arabic scholars.
Ramadan Dawah Training: 20-Day practical skill program covering contemporary issues, public speaking, and intellectual challenges.
Azan Training Program: 15-Day course focusing on proper pronunciation (Makhraj), vocal technique, and Muazzin tarbiyah.
